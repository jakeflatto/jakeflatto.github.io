So, I've long been obsessed with the structure, rules, and bounds of comedy.

What makes a joke funny? How does the medium influence the material? How does that medium evolve over time? How much can you condense a joke by relying on unspoken context? How do you create great comedy without words?

I'm fascinated by these questions and many more, and within the specific interests below, you can learn more about my thoughts on some of these topics.
