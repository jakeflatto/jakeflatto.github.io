## What is it?
Dylan Davies is the name of my solo music project. 
I describe it as "sad boi alt rock, but make it weirdly orchestral."

## Where did I get the name from?
From my actual name! My full name is Jacob Dylan Davies Flatto, 
and I just liked the sound and alliteration of my middle names as a stage name.

## What am I working on?
I'm currently producing a 14-song album that I began writing when I was 18 years old,
entitled:  
**Where We're Going, We Don't Need Roads**

Early in 2026, I released a 3-song EP as a sort of preview for the album,
entitled: **Where We're Going...**
It's comprised of stripped-down, acoustic versions of 3 of the 14 songs from the full album.
