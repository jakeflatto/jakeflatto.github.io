## What is it?
Theory of Solids is the name of my rock band! We play almost entirely original music, 
but we're sure to throw in a few crowd-pleasing covers at any given show.

## Who are we?
I co-founded the band with Stefan Schwartz,
unofficially in about 2010, and officially in about 2019.
We, and our third member, Toma&#769;s Arias, provide guitar, bass, and vocals.
But we are currently seeking a drummer and more guitarists/bassists.
So if you or anyone you know are interested in joining us, please reach out!

## What kind of music do we play?
We describe ourselves as playing rock across the spectrum, 
from folk rock, to indie rock, to alt rock, to metal, 
but we are probably centered right around alt rock.

## Where can I hear your music?
We've been known to play various bars and porchfests around the greater Boston area.
We'll soon be releasing our first EP, containing 4 original songs.
And you can expect at least a couple full albums to be released eventually.

Check back for those streaming links, but in the meantime, check out our socials above!
