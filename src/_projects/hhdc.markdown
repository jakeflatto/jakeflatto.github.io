## What the heck is it?  
The Hollywood Hang Dong Challenge is a silly little movie review platform project I started in 2025. It's mostly a chance for me to practice short-form, creative writing and make countless dick jokes.

But I genuinely do think and hope it has a chance to effect positive change.
My hope is that in time it helps spark conversation, and ultimately shift the narrative/expections
with regards to the gender disparity of nudity among actors.


## Why the heck am I doing it?
I want to make it abundantly clear that I do not support or condone any form of
intimidation, coercion, or pressure for actors of any gender to appear nude when they don't want to.
I think thoughtfully restrained nudity from actors of all genders 
can add a lot to the right scenes and films, 
both within and outside of s\*xual cont\*xts.
But enthusiastic consent on the part of the actors is a crucial part of the thoughtfulness.

The fact is, women are extremely disproportionately asked, if not coerced,
to appear nude when compared to their male counterparts.
It's as though that is the expectation for them, when it is clearly not for men.

This project sets out to change that expectation, one silly review at a time.

So if you like the cause, or you just like weiner jokes, please check it out!
And by all means, feel free to drop a request for a review of your favorite film!
